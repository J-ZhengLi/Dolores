## Completion boundary

Windows Node commands and reviewed MCP servers use LPAC with explicit writable
workspace/read-only grants and zero network capabilities. Additional read-only
roots are a fresh scoped review, even in Full access. Network-enabling grants are
unsupported in this mode; a requested isolated launch never falls back to native.
Human terminals, browser helpers, repair builds and other runtimes retain their
explicit native/unsupported labels. Node-created child pipes remain unsupported;
qualified child processes inherit stdio.

The normal build, focused Rust/UI checks, native command journey, MCP save/restart,
transport/crash controls and separate public-network observations are recorded in
[ACCEPTANCE](../../ACCEPTANCE.md). The earlier investigation entries above retain
failed experiments and then-open gates; this completion record supersedes their
status, not their evidence. Real-model competence, physical desktop input and
clean-machine/platform qualification remain the separate EH10/release boundaries.
